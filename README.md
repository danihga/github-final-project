## Simple Interest Calculator

A Bash script that calculates simple interest on a loan or investment. It prompts you for the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period in years.

### How to use

From the repository folder, run:

```bash
bash simple-interest.sh
```

When prompted, enter the principal, the annual interest rate as a percentage, and the number of years. For a 5% rate, enter `5`.

### Formula

Simple interest = (principal × annual interest rate × time in years) / 100

### Example

For a principal of $1,000 at an annual interest rate of 5% for 2 years, the simple interest is $100.
